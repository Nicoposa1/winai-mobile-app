## Introduction

WinAI ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our WinAI mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Personal Information
- **Account Information**: Email address, name, profile picture
- **Wine Collection Data**: Wine ratings, reviews, cellar information, favorites
- **Device Information**: Device type, operating system, app version
- **Usage Data**: App usage patterns, feature interactions, crash reports

### Automatically Collected Information
- **Analytics Data**: App performance, feature usage, user engagement
- **Camera/Photos**: Only when you explicitly choose to add wine photos (processed locally)
- **Biometric Data**: Stored locally on your device for authentication (never sent to our servers)

## How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
- Provide and maintain the WinAI service
- Personalize wine recommendations using AI
- Sync your wine collection across devices
- Improve app functionality and user experience
- Send important app updates and notifications
- Provide customer support

## Data Storage and Security

- **Secure Storage**: All data is encrypted and stored securely
- **Third-Party Services**: We use Supabase for secure data storage
- **Biometric Data**: Stored locally on your device only
- **No Sale of Data**: We never sell your personal information

## Your Rights

You have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Correct inaccurate information
- Delete your account and data
- Export your data
- Opt-out of communications

## Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:
- **Supabase**: Database and authentication
- **OpenAI**: AI-powered wine recommendations (anonymized data only)
- **Google Sign-In**: Optional authentication method

## Children's Privacy

WinAI is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
